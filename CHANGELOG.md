# Changelog

What's new in Nonet, newest first. The app shows this file under Settings → Changelog, so it is
written for players. Read `docs/CHANGELOG-STYLE.md` before adding to it.

## Unreleased

- **New puzzles.** Every mode and difficulty has 100 new boards, each graded by the steps it takes to solve, with an easier one or a stretch now and then.
- **Your level keeps counting.** Every puzzle you finish adds one, replays included, across all your synced devices, starting from the levels you'd already solved.
- Fixed: a puzzle you left with New puzzle came back right after your next solve; puzzles now come in random order.

## 0.3.1 — 2026-10-14

- Fixed: offline, a puzzle you hadn't opened before said it couldn't load. Every pack is now kept on your device after your first visit.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-14

- **Play on every device.** Turn on sync under Settings → Data, and your scores, settings and games in progress follow you from phone to computer.

## 0.2.2 — 2026-10-14

- **Note Readability Improvements.** Notes show at full strength around the selected square and stay quiet elsewhere; your own notes are warm, Auto notes cool, and all stay readable.
- **Hold Notes to hide every note.** Hold it again, or tap it to pencil, and they come back. On a keyboard, V.
- Fixed: holding a digit on the pad lit it up, but a tap on the selected square still entered the digit before it.

## 0.2.1 — 2026-10-14

- **Notes you can read.** For now, your notes and Auto notes are all one grey, a little bigger and bolder, so a 1 no longer fades into the board.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-13

- **Back up your progress.** Settings → Data saves your scores, settings and games to a file you can restore on another device.
- **Hints go one step at a time.** When a digit needs groundwork first, the hint teaches each step in turn ("Step 1 of 3").
- **Auto notes is a layer.** Dim digits show what each empty square still allows; in notes mode, tap one to strike it.
- With Auto notes on, hints count what it shows, so they don't re-teach a cage sum you can already see.
- Settings has tabs, with the version and this changelog at the bottom.
- Fixed: on narrow phones, the Learn, Scores and Settings buttons ran off the edge of the screen.
